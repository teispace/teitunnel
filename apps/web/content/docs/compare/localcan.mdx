LocalCan is a desktop app for local domains and public URLs, with inspection, snapshots
and an MCP server; it runs its own tunnel service with paid plans. Teitunnel does much of
the same on Cloudflare Tunnel, on your own Cloudflare account, for free.

| | Teitunnel | LocalCan |
|---|---|---|
| Price | free, open source | Free; Solo $8, Pro $12, Teams $45 a month; lifetime $99 |
| Public URLs | as many as you like | Free 1, Solo 5, Pro 10, Teams unlimited |
| Sessions | until you stop them | 60 minutes on Free |
| Traffic | no cap on tunnel traffic | 1 GB a month on Free |
| Your own domain | free, once it's on Cloudflare | Solo 2, Pro 4, Teams unlimited |
| Local HTTPS domains | `.localhost` and `.test` ([local domains](/docs/guides/local-domains)) | `.local` |
| Snapshots | on your Cloudflare account ([Snapshots](/docs/guides/snapshots)) | 150 MB on Free |
| Protection | passwords, secret links, tokens, networks; logins with Cloudflare Access | passwords and IP rules on paid plans |
| AI agents | MCP server, changes wait for your approval by default | MCP server |
| Platforms | macOS, Windows, Linux, servers, Docker | Mac, Windows, Linux |

<Callout title="Checked 2026-09-26">
  From [LocalCan's pricing](https://www.localcan.com/pricing). LocalCan's pages are the
  final word.
</Callout>

## When LocalCan fits better

- You want `.local` names that phones on your Wi-Fi can open.
- You'd rather pay for a hosted service than keep a domain on Cloudflare.

## When Teitunnel fits better

- You already have a domain on Cloudflare, or want no plan limits.
- You want every change to your DNS and tunnels shown first and undoable
  ([Changes](/docs/concepts/changes)).
- You also run servers: the same CLI and routes work on Linux servers and in Docker.
