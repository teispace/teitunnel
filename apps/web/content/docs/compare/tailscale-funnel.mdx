---
title: Teitunnel vs Tailscale Funnel
description: Teitunnel and Tailscale Funnel compared — ports, custom domains, logins, inspection and bandwidth — and when each fits.
---

Tailscale Funnel makes a service on a Tailscale device reachable from the public internet
at a `ts.net` name. It's part of Tailscale, so it's a natural choice when your devices are
already on a tailnet. Teitunnel is an app for Cloudflare Tunnel, reachable at a random
address or on your own domain.

| | Teitunnel | Tailscale Funnel |
|---|---|---|
| Price | free, open source | on every Tailscale plan |
| Names | random `trycloudflare.com`, or any name on your domain | `*.ts.net` names in your tailnet only |
| Public ports | 443 (HTTPS); TCP services through Cloudflare | 443, 8443 and 10000 |
| Bandwidth | no cap on tunnel traffic | "non-configurable bandwidth limits" |
| Set up | the app, or a Cloudflare account for your own domain | Tailscale 1.38.3+, MagicDNS, HTTPS certificates, a `funnel` attribute in the policy file |
| Logins for visitors | [Cloudflare Access](/docs/guides/require-login), free up to 50 users | none: Funnel is public; Tailscale Serve keeps it private to the tailnet |
| Inspect and replay | the [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector) | no |
| Desktop app | macOS, Windows, Linux | Tailscale's app; Funnel is set up from the CLI |

<Callout title="Checked 2026-09-26">
  From [Tailscale Funnel's documentation](https://tailscale.com/kb/1223/funnel). Tailscale's
  pages are the final word.
</Callout>

## When Funnel fits better

- Your devices are on a tailnet already, and a `ts.net` name is fine.
- You want one tool for private access (Serve) and the occasional public share (Funnel).

## When Teitunnel fits better

- You want your own domain, or several names for one computer.
- Visitors should sign in, or machines should pass with a
  [service token](/docs/guides/protection#service-tokens-for-machines).
- You want to see and replay requests, share a folder, or keep a
  [static copy](/docs/guides/snapshots) online while the computer is off.

Both can run on the same computer: a Funnel on one port and a Teitunnel route on another.
