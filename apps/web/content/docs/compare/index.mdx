Teitunnel is a free, open-source app for Cloudflare Tunnel. It doesn't run tunnels of its
own: shares and routes go through Cloudflare, on your own Cloudflare account (or on
Cloudflare's no-account Quick Tunnels). Other tools run their own service and sell plans
for it. That one difference explains most of the table.

## Where Teitunnel fits

- **You want your own domain without a subscription.** A Cloudflare account and a domain
  on it are enough; Cloudflare doesn't charge for traffic through a tunnel
  ([Limits and costs](/docs/reference/limits)).
- **You want to see every change first.** Tunnels, DNS records, logins and rules are shown
  as a plan, applied with rollback, and can be undone from Activity
  ([Changes](/docs/concepts/changes)). Records you made yourself are never deleted
  ([DNS ownership](/docs/concepts/dns-ownership)).
- **You debug on your own computer.** The [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector), request
  replay, breakpoints and mocks run locally; captured traffic doesn't leave the machine.

## Where something else fits better

- **Your domain isn't on Cloudflare and won't move.** Routes need a domain whose
  nameservers point to Cloudflare. Quick Shares work without one, with a random address.
- **Public UDP.** Cloudflare Tunnel carries HTTP and TCP to the public; UDP only reaches
  [private networks](/docs/guides/private-networks) through WARP. Pinggy has UDP tunnels.
- **Everyone is already on one tailnet.** Tailscale Serve, without Funnel, keeps a service
  private to your devices with nothing else to set up.
