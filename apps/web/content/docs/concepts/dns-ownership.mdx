---
title: DNS ownership
description: Which DNS records Teitunnel may change, and why it asks before touching the rest.
---

Your DNS is shared with everything else you run, so Teitunnel keeps track of which
records it created.

- Every record Teitunnel creates carries a comment, `teitunnel:route=<id>`, and is also
  remembered locally.
- **Only those records are ever changed or deleted without asking.** Removing a route
  removes its record only if Teitunnel created it.
- Replacing or deleting any other record is shown in the plan, with the existing record,
  and needs your explicit confirmation.

The Doctor looks for records that point at a tunnel which no longer exists, or at this
Mac's tunnel without a route. Those show a Cloudflare error to visitors, and a record left
dangling is a subdomain-takeover risk, so it offers to delete them. **Fix Safe Issues** only
ever touches records Teitunnel created.
