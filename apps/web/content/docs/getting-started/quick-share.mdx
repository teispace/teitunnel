A **Quick Share** gives a service on your computer, such as a dev server on `localhost:3000`,
a public HTTPS address like `https://quiet-river-lamp-orbit.trycloudflare.com`. Anyone with
the link can open it until you stop sharing. It needs no Cloudflare account, no domain, no
port forwarding and no configuration.

**Use it** to show work in progress to a colleague or client, open your site on a phone,
or point a webhook at your laptop for a few minutes. **Use something else** when the address
has to stay the same or stay up: a [share on your own domain](#on-your-own-domain) keeps its
name, a [route](/docs/getting-started/first-route) runs for as long as you like, and a
[Snapshot](/docs/guides/snapshots) stays online when your computer is off. See
[choose how to share](/docs/concepts/ways-to-share) for all of them side by side.

## Share in the app

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Open Quick Share

    Choose **Quick Share** in the sidebar (⌘3 on macOS, Ctrl+3 on Windows and Linux) or
    **New Quick Share** (⌘⇧N, Ctrl+Shift+N). From the menu bar or tray icon,
    **Share a Local Port…** opens the same thing.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Pick what to share

    Click **Port or address**: Teitunnel lists what's **Running on this Mac** (*this PC* on
    Windows): dev servers such as Vite, Next.js, Astro, Django, Rails and Laravel, and Docker
    containers, with their project names. Pick one, or type a port or an address such as
    `3000`, `localhost:5173` or `https://localhost:8443`.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose when it stops

    **Keep running** (until you stop it or quit Teitunnel), **Stop after 15 min**,
    **Stop after 1 hour** or **Stop after 8 hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Share

    Choose **Share**. Teitunnel first runs the [exposure check](/docs/guides/exposure-check),
    which looks for a `.env` file, a `.git` folder or debug pages the service would hand
    out. If it finds one, choose **Share Anyway** or **Cancel**. The card then shows
    *Getting a URL…*, and **Live** with the address a few seconds later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Screenshot name="quick-share" alt="Quick Share: a live share with its trycloudflare.com URL, request count and log" />

Each share's card has:

- the address, with the copy button and **Open in browser**,
- **Show QR code**, to open it on a phone,
- request and error counts, *Stops in …* when it has a time limit, and the connector's
  **Log**,
- **Pause** (when **Inspect requests** is on, the default), which keeps the address but shows
  visitors a paused page until you **Resume**,
- **Publish a Snapshot**, which turns the shared site into a static copy that stays online,
- **Stop Sharing**.

Quitting Teitunnel stops every Quick Share, and Cloudflare forgets the address. Sharing again
gives a new one.

## From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel share 3000                  # a random trycloudflare.com URL
teitunnel share 3000 --for 30m        # stops by itself after 30 minutes
teitunnel share ./dist                # a folder of files, no server needed
teitunnel share 3000 --json           # {"url": …, "hostname": …} for scripts
```

```text
$ teitunnel share 3000 --here
https://quiet-river-lamp-orbit.trycloudflare.com

█▀▀▀▀▀█ ▄▀▄ ▀▄█ █▀▀▀▀▀█
…
http://localhost:3000 is public at https://quiet-river-lamp-orbit.trycloudflare.com for anyone with the link. Press Ctrl-C to stop.
```

Where the share runs depends on whether the app is open:

- **The app is running:** the command hands the share to the app (the app may ask you to
  allow it the first time) and returns. The share keeps running in the app:
  *It runs in the Teitunnel app: stop it there or with `teitunnel shares --stop <url>`.*
- **The app isn't running,** or you pass `--here`: the share runs in the terminal until you
  press Ctrl-C or its `--for` time is up.

`share` needs no Cloudflare account, only cloudflared (the app's copy, or one on your
system). Every option is in [Sharing](/docs/guides/sharing#share-from-a-terminal) and the
[command reference](/docs/reference/cli#share).

## On your own domain

With a Cloudflare account connected (see [your first route](/docs/getting-started/first-route)),
the **Address** menu next to the service also offers your domains. Choose
**On yourhost.com**, type a **Subdomain** such as `demo` (Teitunnel suggests names from the
project), and **Share**. You get `https://demo.yourhost.com`, the same address every time you
share under that name.

<Screenshot name="share-on-domain" alt="Quick Share with the Address menu set to a domain, the subdomain demo typed in, suggested names from the project and the Host header option under Advanced" />

It's a temporary [route](/docs/concepts/routes-and-tunnels) on this computer's tunnel,
planned and applied like any other change, and removed with its DNS record when you stop it,
when its time is up, or when you quit Teitunnel (if Teitunnel crashes, it's removed the next
time it starts). It never takes over a hostname that has a DNS record Teitunnel didn't create.
While it runs, it shows in **Routes** with a *Temporary* badge.

```sh
teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.yourhost.com
teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.yourhost.com --allow ana@yourhost.com   # behind a login
```

A share on your domain always runs in the terminal that started it, until Ctrl-C.

|  | Random address | On your domain |
|---|---|---|
| Needs | Nothing | A Cloudflare account and a domain on it (free plan is fine) |
| Address | New every time | The one you choose, every time |
| Cloudflare's limits | Quick Tunnel limits (below) | Your plan's, like any route |
| Logins, schedules | No | Yes: [require a login](/docs/guides/require-login), [schedules](/docs/guides/sharing#on-a-schedule) |

## Limits

<Callout type="info">
Random `trycloudflare.com` addresses are Cloudflare's free **Quick Tunnels**, meant for
testing and demos. Cloudflare doesn't guarantee their uptime, they carry at most 200
requests in flight at once, and they don't carry server-sent events. For anything lasting,
or for event streams, share on your own domain. See [limits](/docs/reference/limits#quick-shares)
and [Cloudflare's Quick Tunnels documentation](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/do-more-with-tunnels/trycloudflare/).
</Callout>

Anyone who has the link can open the service while it's shared. To limit who, share on your
domain with a login, or put a password page or a secret link in front of it from the
shield on the share's card ([protect a Quick Share](/docs/guides/sharing#protect-a-quick-share)).

## If it doesn't work

- **"Cloudflare didn't provide a URL in time. Check your internet connection and try
  again."** Quick Tunnels are sometimes busy. Try again, or share on your own domain.
- **The page says "Blocked request" or "Invalid Host header".** Vite, webpack, Angular,
  Django and Rails refuse hostnames they don't know. Teitunnel sends dev servers their own
  address by default; if yours still refuses, see
  [share a dev server](/docs/tutorials/dev-servers).
- **502 Bad Gateway, or nothing loads.** Check the service answers at
  `http://localhost:<port>` on your own machine, and that the port is right. See
  [troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting#502-bad-gateway).
- **"This Quick Share hit its limit of 200 requests at once. Share on your own domain for
  more."** Quick Tunnels have that cap; a share on your domain doesn't.
