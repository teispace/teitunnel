Teitunnel's window has a sidebar of sections, each a list on the left and the selected item's
details on the right. This page says what each section is for, which command does the same in
a terminal, and how to get around with the keyboard.

<Screenshot name="overview" alt="Teitunnel's Overview: problems to look at, traffic, errors and uptime on this machine, routes with their status and Quick Shares" />

## The sidebar

### Your work

| Section | What it's for | In a terminal |
|---|---|---|
| **Overview** | What's running now: problems that need you, traffic, errors and uptime on this computer, your routes and Quick Shares, and recent requests. | `teitunnel status`, `teitunnel top` |
| **Routes** | Hostnames on your domains and the services they reach. Select one for its address, details, activity, logs, pause and schedule, edge protection, offline page and webhook inbox. [First route](/docs/getting-started/first-route) | `teitunnel routes`, `teitunnel route add` |
| **Quick Share** | Temporary public addresses, random or on your domain, for a service or a folder. [Sharing](/docs/guides/sharing) | `teitunnel share`, `teitunnel shares` |
| **Inspector** | Every request to inspected shares and routes: see, replay, edit, compare and export. [Inspector](/docs/guides/inspector) | `teitunnel traffic` |
| **Snapshots** | Static copies of a site, hosted on your Cloudflare account, that stay online when your computer is off. [Snapshots](/docs/guides/snapshots) | `teitunnel snapshot` |
| **Projects** | `teitunnel.yml` files: what a repository declares, and whether it's applied. [Project files](/docs/guides/project-file) | `teitunnel project` |
| **Local Domains** | HTTPS names like `shop.test` for services on this computer, never on the internet. [Local domains](/docs/guides/local-domains) | `teitunnel local-domain` |
| **Comments** | Feedback visitors pinned on shared pages and Snapshots. [Comments](/docs/guides/comments) | `teitunnel comments` |
| **AI & Integrations** | AI apps and agents using Teitunnel's MCP server, MCP servers you share, the browser extension, editor extensions and `teitunnel://` links. [AI agents](/docs/guides/ai-agents), [Integrations](/docs/guides/integrations) | `teitunnel mcp` |

### Cloudflare

| Section | What it's for | In a terminal |
|---|---|---|
| **Domains** | The domains of your connected accounts: whether each is *Active* or *Waiting for nameservers* (with the nameservers to set), its plan, what the credential may do on it, and its [reservations](/docs/concepts/dns-ownership#reservations). | `teitunnel reservations` |
| **Tunnels** | This computer's tunnels and others in the account: whether each runs here, **Keep running when Teitunnel quits**, connectors, private networks, traffic and logs. [Run modes](/docs/concepts/run-modes), [Several tunnels](/docs/guides/several-tunnels) | `teitunnel tunnels`, `teitunnel always-on` |

### Health

| Section | What it's for | In a terminal |
|---|---|---|
| **Activity** | Every change Teitunnel made to Cloudflare, step by step: its outcome, the agent that asked for it, the same steps as commands, and a check that it still works. [Changes](/docs/concepts/changes#activity) | none |
| **Doctor** | Problems Teitunnel found, why they happen, and fixes you apply in one click. [Doctor](/docs/reference/doctor) | `teitunnel doctor` |
| **Analytics** | Requests, errors, response times and countries from Cloudflare's edge, uptime checks and alerts, per route. [Analytics](/docs/guides/analytics) | `teitunnel analytics`, `teitunnel uptime` |

**Settings** is at the bottom of the sidebar (and ⌘, / Ctrl+,). A number next to **Doctor**
counts problems; one next to **Comments** counts unread comments.

## Keyboard shortcuts

⌘ on macOS is Ctrl on Windows and Linux.

| Keys | Does |
|---|---|
| ⌘1 … ⌘9 | Overview, Routes, Quick Share, Snapshots, Domains, Tunnels, Activity, Doctor, Analytics |
| ⌘N | New Route |
| ⇧⌘N | New Quick Share |
| ⌘R | Refresh |
| ⌘K | Command palette |
| ⌥⌘S | Show or hide the sidebar |
| ⌥⌘I | Show or hide the details pane on the right |
| ⌘, | Settings |
| ⌘Q (Ctrl+Q) | Quit. If routes would stop, Teitunnel asks first and offers to keep them running. |

Inspector, Projects, Local Domains, Comments and AI & Integrations have no number: open them
from the sidebar or the command palette.

A **global shortcut** that shares your dev server from any app is off by default; turn it on
in **AI & Integrations ▸ More** ([menu bar and shortcut](/docs/guides/menu-bar#the-global-shortcut)).

## The command palette

⌘K (Ctrl+K), or **View ▸ Command Palette…** on macOS, opens a search over everything above:
type a few letters of a section or an action and press Return. Besides the sections, it has
**New Route**, **New Quick Share**, **Refresh**, **Settings**, **Export Diagnostics**,
**Toggle Sidebar**, **Toggle Inspector**, and links to this documentation, Cloudflare Tunnel's
documentation, the release notes and **Report an Issue…**.

## The menu bar or tray icon

Closing the window keeps routes and Quick Shares running. The menu bar icon (the tray icon on
Windows and Linux) shows whether your routes are live and lists your shares, with
**Share a Local Port…**, **Pause All Shares** and **Stop Routes on This Mac**. Quitting
Teitunnel from it, or with ⌘Q, stops what isn't set to keep running. See
[menu bar and tray](/docs/guides/menu-bar).

## Settings

| Tab | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **General** | Appearance, updates, **Open at login**, installing the `teitunnel` **Command line**, notifications and quiet hours, alert rules, the check **Before Sharing**, API descriptions from captured requests, **Move to Another Computer**, and the menu bar icon. |
| **Accounts** | Connected Cloudflare accounts, what each may do (**Permissions**), and **Connect an Account**. [Accounts](/docs/guides/accounts) |
| **Inspector** | Defaults for inspection: inspecting Quick Shares, stopping idle shares, what's kept and for how long. [Inspector](/docs/guides/inspector) |
| **cloudflared** | The connector's version, where it came from, where it is, and its updates. [Run modes](/docs/concepts/run-modes#cloudflared-updates) |

## When something needs you

- **Notifications** say when a route goes down or comes back, when the Doctor finds a problem,
  when a share stops, and when an alert fires. They only appear when no Teitunnel window is in
  front. Choose which in **Settings ▸ General ▸ Notifications**
  ([notifications](/docs/guides/observability#notifications)).
- **Reviews**: every change to Cloudflare opens a sheet with the plan. Nothing changes until
  you apply it ([how changes work](/docs/concepts/changes)).
- **Approvals**: when the `teitunnel` command, an editor extension or an AI agent asks the app
  to do something, the app asks you first ([integrations](/docs/guides/integrations),
  [AI agents](/docs/guides/ai-agents#approvals-in-the-app)).
