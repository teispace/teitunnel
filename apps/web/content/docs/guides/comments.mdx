---
title: Comments
description: "Let reviewers pin comments to any spot on a share or Snapshot, reply and resolve; read and answer them in Teitunnel, the terminal or from an AI agent."
---

Send someone a link and get feedback exactly where it applies. With **Comments** on, the
shared pages get a small comment button in the corner. Reviewers click a spot on the page,
type, and the comment is pinned there with a number; they can reply to each other and
resolve threads. You read and answer everything in Teitunnel's **Comments** view.

## Turn it on
- **A Quick Share or a share on your domain:** on its card in **Quick Share**, switch on
  **Comments**. It works while requests go through the [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector)
  (the default).
- **A route:** while the route is being inspected, the same switch appears for it.
- **A Snapshot:** when you publish it (or a new version), switch on **Comments** in the
  settings, or pass `--comments` to `teitunnel snapshot publish`.

## For reviewers
The button shows how many threads are open. **Add a comment**, then click the spot (or
**Comment on the whole page**), type, and press **Comment** (⌘/Ctrl-Enter works too).
Reviewers give their name once; their browser remembers it. When the hostname has a
[login](/docs/guides/require-login), the name comes from Cloudflare Access and the
comment is marked **Signed in**.

The overlay is keyboard accessible, follows the page's light or dark system setting and
Reduce Motion, and lives in its own Shadow DOM, so it never picks up or changes the page's
styles. It loads nothing from anywhere else, and works with a Content Security Policy
that allows scripts from the site itself.

## Read and answer
**Comments** in the sidebar lists every share and Snapshot with comments, with a badge for
new ones; a notification tells you when someone writes. Each thread shows its page, the
comments, and:

- **Reply**: your answer is marked as yours for reviewers.
- **Resolve** / **Reopen**.
- **Show on Page**: opens the page in your browser at the spot, with the thread open.

From the terminal:

```sh
teitunnel comments ls
teitunnel comments ls preview.example.com
teitunnel comments reply preview.example.com c1a2b3 "Fixed, thanks"
teitunnel comments resolve preview.example.com c1a2b3
```

AI agents connected with [MCP](/docs/guides/ai-agents) get `comments_list`,
`comments_reply` and `comments_resolve`: an agent can read the feedback on a preview,
change the code and answer. Replies and resolutions ask you first, like every change.
Reviewers' email addresses are never given to agents.

## Where comments are kept
- **Shares and routes:** in Teitunnel's database on this computer. Nothing goes to
  Cloudflare, and it works on Quick Shares without an account (the page is only
  reachable while this computer is on anyway).
- **Snapshots:** in a **D1 database** on your own Cloudflare account (`teitunnel-data`,
  created the first time you turn comments on, through the reviewed plan). The Snapshot's
  Worker answers reviewers; Teitunnel reads and answers with your API token. Deleting a
  Snapshot deletes its comments.

## Limits
A comment is at most 4,000 characters, a name 80. A share or Snapshot keeps up to 2,000
comments and a thread 200. Each visitor can write 10 comments a minute and 60 an hour.
Comments are stored and shown as plain text: HTML or scripts in them are never run.

With comments on, every request to a Snapshot runs its Worker (to add the comment button),
which counts toward Cloudflare's free 100,000 Worker requests a day. D1's free plan
includes 5 million rows read and 100,000 written a day.

## Permissions
Snapshot comments need **Account · D1 · Edit** on your API token, besides the Workers
permissions Snapshots already use. If it's missing, Teitunnel shows how to add it to the
same token.
