---
title: Comments
description: "Let reviewers pin comments to any spot on a share or Snapshot, reply and resolve; read and answer them in Teitunnel, the terminal or from an AI agent."
---

Send someone a link and get feedback exactly where it applies. With **Comments** on, the
shared pages get a small comment button in the corner. Reviewers click a spot on the page,
type, and the comment is pinned there with a number; they can reply to each other and
resolve threads. You read and answer everything in Teitunnel's **Comments** view, from the
terminal, or through an AI agent.

Use it for design reviews, a client looking at a preview, or a teammate testing a branch:
anything where "the button on the pricing page" is easier to point at than to describe.

## Turn it on

- **A Quick Share, or a share on your domain:** on its card in **Quick Share**, switch on
  **Comments**. "Reviewers can now comment on this share's pages". The switch is there
  while the share's requests go through the [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector), which is
  the default; the inspector adds the comment button to the pages.
- **A share from the terminal:** `teitunnel share 5173 --comments` (or with
  `--on demo.teispace.com`); the share runs in that terminal.
- **A Snapshot:** when you publish it or a new version, switch on **Comments** in the
  settings step, or pass `--comments` to `teitunnel snapshot publish` or `update`.
  `--no-comments` turns them off again.

**Show Comments** next to the switch opens the Comments view at that share.

## For reviewers

The button in the corner shows how many threads are open.

1. Choose **Add a comment**, then click the spot you want to comment on (or choose
   **Comment on the whole page**).
2. Type the comment and press **Comment** (⌘-Enter or Ctrl-Enter works too).
3. The first time, give **Your name**; the browser remembers it.

Reviewers can **Reply**, **Resolve** and **Reopen** threads, and **Show resolved** to see
closed ones. When the hostname has Teitunnel's own [login](/docs/guides/require-login), the
name comes from Cloudflare Access and the comment is marked **Signed in**. Your own replies
are marked **Owner**.

The overlay is keyboard accessible, follows the visitor's light or dark setting and Reduce
Motion, and lives in a closed Shadow DOM, so it never picks up or changes the page's
styles. It loads nothing from anywhere else, and works under a strict Content Security
Policy.

## Read and answer

**Comments** in the sidebar lists every share and Snapshot with comments ("Shares with
comments"), with a badge for unread ones. A notification ("New comment on
preview.teispace.com") tells you when someone writes, except during quiet hours. Each
thread shows its page and the comments, with:

- **Reply**: your answer is marked as the owner's for reviewers.
- **Resolve** / **Reopen**.
- **Show on Page**: opens the page in your browser at the spot, with the thread open.
- **Open Page**: opens the page.

Use **Show** to switch between **Open** and **Resolved** threads.

Comments on live shares arrive at once. Comments on Snapshots are read from Cloudflare
every 2 minutes while the app runs.

## From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel comments ls                                  # what has comments
teitunnel comments ls preview.teispace.com             # its open threads
teitunnel comments ls "Launch page" --all              # include resolved threads
teitunnel comments reply preview.teispace.com c1a2b3 "Fixed in the new version, thanks"
teitunnel comments resolve preview.teispace.com c1a2b3
teitunnel comments resolve preview.teispace.com c1a2b3 --reopen
```

A subject is a hostname, a Snapshot's name, or a key from `comments ls` (`snapshot:…`,
`route:…`, `share:…`). Each thread is listed with its id, page and a link that opens it on
the page. `--json` prints JSON. Replies are written as you, the owner.

## From an AI agent

Agents connected with [MCP](/docs/guides/ai-agents) get `comments_list`, `comments_reply`
and `comments_resolve`: an agent can read the feedback on a preview, change the code and
answer. Replies and resolutions are changes, so they ask you first in `ask` mode.
Reviewers' email addresses are never given to agents.

## Where comments are kept

- **Shares:** in Teitunnel's database on this computer. Nothing goes to Cloudflare, it costs
  no Worker requests, and it works on Quick Shares without an account. The pages are only
  reachable while this computer is on anyway.
- **Snapshots:** in a **D1 database** on your own Cloudflare account, `teitunnel-data`,
  created the first time you turn comments on, as a step of the reviewed plan. The
  Snapshot's Worker answers reviewers; Teitunnel reads and answers with your own API token,
  so no Worker has an owner API or holds a key. Deleting a Snapshot deletes its comments.
  The same database holds [webhook inboxes](/docs/guides/webhook-inbox).

## Limits and costs

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| One comment | 4,000 characters |
| A name | 80 characters |
| Comments per share or Snapshot | 2,000 |
| Comments per thread | 200 |
| Writes per visitor | 10 a minute, 60 an hour |
| Request body | 16 KB |

Comments are stored and shown as plain text: HTML or scripts in them are never run, and
control and bidi-override characters are refused. Comments can only be posted from a page
of the same site.

With comments on, every request to a Snapshot runs its Worker (to add the comment button),
which counts toward Cloudflare's free 100,000 Worker requests a day. D1's free plan
includes 5 million rows read and 100,000 rows written a day, and 5 GB of storage.

## Permissions

Snapshot comments need **Account · D1 · Edit**, besides the Workers permissions Snapshots
already use. If it's missing, Teitunnel shows what to add ("To keep Snapshot comments and
webhooks in a database on your account.") and continues when you're back. Comments on
shares need no permissions.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="There's no Comments switch on a share">
The switch is shown while the share goes through the inspector. If you turned the
inspector off for this share, turn it on again (a Quick Share restarts with a new address).
Local domains have no comments.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Comments aren't on for this Snapshot. Publish a new version with comments on.">
Publish a new version with **Comments** on (or `teitunnel snapshot update <name>
--settings-only --comments`).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Connect the Snapshot's Cloudflare account to read its comments.">
Snapshot comments are read with the account's token. Connect the account in
**Accounts**, then refresh.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Too many comments at once. Try again in a minute.">
A reviewer hit the limit of 10 comments a minute or 60 an hour.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="There are too many comments here. Resolve and clear some first.">
The share or Snapshot reached 2,000 comments, or the thread 200.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Couldn't reach the comments on Cloudflare: …">
Cloudflare didn't answer, or the D1 free daily limit was used up. It resets at 00:00 UTC.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
