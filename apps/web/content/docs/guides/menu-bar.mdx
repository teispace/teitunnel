---
title: The menu bar and tray
description: Status and quick actions without opening the window.
---

On macOS Teitunnel has a menu bar icon; on Windows it's in the notification area and on
Linux in the system tray (left click opens the app on Windows, right click shows the menu).
The icon shows a dot when a route isn't working. Its menu has:

- a **health line** ("All 3 routes live", "1 of 3 routes not working"),
- each **route** with its status, to copy its URL or open it,
- **Stop Routes on This Mac** / **Start Routes on This Mac** (*This PC* on Windows, *This Computer* on Linux),
- your **Quick Shares**, with **Share a Local Port…**,
- **Open Teitunnel** and **Quit Teitunnel**.

Stopping routes yourself doesn't count as a problem, so the icon stays plain.

Closing the window leaves Teitunnel running in the menu bar. **Settings ▸ General ▸ Open
at login** starts it there when you log in, without opening the window. You can hide the
icon in **Settings ▸ General ▸ Menu bar**.
