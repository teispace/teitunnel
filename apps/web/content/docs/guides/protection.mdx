title: Edge protection
description: "Challenge or block bots and AI crawlers, rate limit visitors, change headers, and give machines service tokens: rules Cloudflare enforces for one hostname, planned and undoable like every change."
---

A login ([Require a login](/docs/guides/require-login)) decides **who** may open a
hostname. **Edge protection** decides what else happens to its traffic at Cloudflare,
before it reaches your computer:

- **Automated clients:** allow, **challenge** or **block** scripts, command-line tools and
  headless browsers. Verified bots such as search engines are let through.
- **AI crawlers:** block Cloudflare's verified AI crawlers.
- **Rate limit:** at most so many requests per period per visitor (IP address), then block
  or challenge them until the period ends. Pro plans and up.
- **Header rules:** set or remove request headers before they reach your service, and set,
  add or remove response headers before they reach visitors (for example
  `X-Robots-Tag: noindex` on a preview).
- **Service tokens:** let machines (CI, scripts, other servers) through a login with two
  headers.

Cloudflare enforces these rules, so they keep working while this computer is off, and
blocked traffic never reaches it. Every rule applies to **one hostname** only: Teitunnel
never changes anything for a whole domain. That's why it doesn't offer Bot Fight Mode or
"Block AI bots", which are domain-wide switches in the dashboard.

## Protect a hostname in the app

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Edit Protection…**, from any of:

- a route in **Routes**, in its **Edge Protection** section ("Enforced by Cloudflare for
  this hostname only, so it keeps working while this computer is off.");
- the route's **Edit Route** sheet, under **Advanced**;
- the shield button (**Protect at Cloudflare**) on a share on your domain.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Protect app.teispace.com**, choose:

- **Automated clients:** **Allow**, **Challenge** or **Block**.
- **Block AI crawlers.**
- **Rate limit:** turn on **Limit requests per visitor**, then **Requests** per **Period**
  and what happens **Above the limit** (**Block** or **Challenge**).
- **Header rules:** **Add Header** under **Request headers (before they reach your
  service)** or **Response headers (before they reach visitors)**, each with **Set**,
  **Add** (response only) or **Remove**.

**Rules on teispace.com** shows how many custom rules, rate limiting rules and Transform
Rules the domain uses of its plan's quota.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Review**, read the plan, and choose **Apply Rules** (**Remove Rules** when you
turn everything off). The notification "Edge protection updated for app.teispace.com" has
**Undo**, which puts the previous settings back.
</Step>
</Steps>

## From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel protect app.teispace.com                     # what it has now, and the quotas
teitunnel protect app.teispace.com --bots challenge --block-ai
teitunnel protect app.teispace.com --rate-limit 30/1m  # Pro plans and up
teitunnel protect app.teispace.com --rate-limit 100/10s:challenge
teitunnel protect preview.teispace.com --set-response-header X-Robots-Tag:noindex
teitunnel protect api.teispace.com --remove-request-header Cookie
teitunnel protect app.teispace.com --off               # remove every rule Teitunnel added
```

| Flag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `--bots off\|challenge\|block` | Automated clients. |
| `--block-ai` / `--allow-ai` | Block AI crawlers, or stop blocking them. |
| `--rate-limit N/PERIOD[:ACTION]` | Requests per period per visitor; periods `10s`, `1m`, `2m`, `5m`, `10m`, `1h`; action `block` (default) or `challenge`. `--no-rate-limit` removes it. |
| `--set-request-header NAME:VALUE` | Set a header on requests. |
| `--remove-request-header NAME` | Remove a header from requests. |
| `--set-response-header NAME:VALUE` | Set a header on responses. |
| `--add-response-header NAME:VALUE` | Add a header to responses, keeping existing values. |
| `--remove-response-header NAME` | Remove a header from responses. |
| `--clear-headers` | Start from no header rules, then apply the header options given. |
| `--off` | Remove every rule Teitunnel added for the hostname. |
| `-a, --account`, `-y, --yes`, `--json` | Account, apply without asking, JSON output. |

Options you don't give keep their current value; header options add to or replace (by
name) the current header rules. The hostname is a route, or a share on your domain.

## What happens on Cloudflare

The plan ("Protect app.teispace.com at Cloudflare's edge") lists one step per rule, for
example "Challenge automated clients on app.teispace.com", "Block visitors who send
app.teispace.com more than 30 requests in 60 seconds" or "Change 1 response header on
preview.teispace.com". After the change it shows the domain's quotas as they'll be.

Teitunnel adds, changes and removes only its own rules, one at a time, and never replaces a
whole rule list:

| Feature | Cloudflare rule | Phase |
|---|---|---|
| Automated clients, AI crawlers | Custom rule (WAF) | `http_request_firewall_custom` |
| Rate limit | Rate limiting rule | `http_ratelimit` |
| Request headers | Transform Rule | `http_request_late_transform` |
| Response headers | Transform Rule | `http_response_headers_transform` |

Every rule matches `http.host eq "<hostname>"` and is marked `teitunnel:` in its
description; Teitunnel also remembers the rules' ids. Rules you or others made in the
dashboard are counted toward the quotas but never changed, moved or removed.

"Automated clients" means a request that isn't from a verified bot and has the user agent
of an automated client (empty, `curl`, `wget`, `python-requests`, headless browsers and
similar). Bot scores are an Enterprise feature, so Teitunnel doesn't use them.

## Your plan's limits

Cloudflare counts rules per domain, and Teitunnel shows how many are used (yours and
others') before and after each change:

| | Free | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Custom rules (bots, AI crawlers) | 5 | 20 | 100 |
| Rate limiting rules | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| Rate limit periods | 10 s | up to 1 min | up to 10 min |
| Transform Rules (headers, URL rewrites) | 10 | 25 | 50 |

Each protected hostname uses at most two custom rules and one Transform Rule per direction.
A header list holds up to 20 rules.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rate limits need Pro or higher">
On the Free plan, the one rate limiting rule can't match a hostname (Cloudflare only lets it
match paths there), so it would apply to the whole domain. Teitunnel refuses instead of
doing that. On Pro and higher, hostnames with the **same limit share one rule**, so a second
hostname doesn't use up the quota. If the plan has no room for another limit, the plan says
which hostnames use the existing one and what it is: use the same limit, or remove it from
them first.
</Callout>

## Service tokens

A login stops people who aren't allowed, and also CI jobs, scripts and other servers. A
**service token** lets a machine through with two headers:

```sh
curl https://api.teispace.com/health \
  -H "CF-Access-Client-Id: $CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_ID" \
  -H "CF-Access-Client-Secret: $CF_ACCESS_CLIENT_SECRET"
```

### Create one in the app

1. Open the route in **Routes**. Under **Service Tokens**, choose **New Service Token…**.
2. Give it a **Name**: what it's for, e.g. `CI` or `Backup script` (1 to 40 characters).
3. Review the plan and choose **Create Token**.
4. **Save the Secret Now** shows the `CF-Access-Client-Id`; the secret is copied without
   being displayed. Use **Copy Both Headers** and paste them into your CI's secrets.

Cloudflare shows a secret only once, and Teitunnel doesn't keep it: the app holds it in
memory for 10 minutes, and stores only the token's id and client id. If you miss it,
**Rotate** the token for a new secret.

If the hostname has no login yet, it gets one that **only service tokens pass** ("Require a
login on app.teispace.com that only service tokens pass"): people opening it in a browser
are refused, and the plan warns you. Tokens are valid for a year; the list shows when each
expires. **Rotate** ("Give "CI" a new secret?") replaces the secret, and the old one stops
working at once. **Revoke** takes the token out of the login and deletes it; machines
using it can no longer reach the hostname. Changing who may log in never removes the
tokens.

### From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel service-token create api.teispace.com --name CI   # prints both headers once
teitunnel service-token ls api.teispace.com
teitunnel service-token rotate api.teispace.com CI           # a new secret, printed once
teitunnel service-token revoke api.teispace.com CI
```

A token is named by its name or id. `create` defaults to the name `CLI`.

Service tokens are part of Cloudflare Zero Trust, which must be set up once on the account
(free for up to 50 users; 50 service tokens per account). Teitunnel changes only tokens it
created.

## Permissions

Edge rules need **Zone · Zone WAF · Edit** and **Zone · Transform Rules · Edit** on the
domain; service tokens need **Account · Access: Service Tokens · Edit**. Tokens created from
Teitunnel's link and OAuth sign-in ask for them; a credential without them gets the usual
in-place fix that names what to add.

## AI agents

Agents use `get_protection`, `protect_hostname` (a plan, applied with `apply_plan` after
your approval), `service_token_list`, `service_token_create` and `service_token_revoke`
([AI agents](/docs/guides/ai-agents)). A token an agent creates is returned to it once,
after you approved it, and marked sensitive.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="… is on Cloudflare's Free plan, whose rate limit can't be limited to one hostname">
Rate limiting per hostname needs Pro or higher. On Free, use **Automated clients:
Challenge** instead, or a [login](/docs/guides/require-login).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="… already uses all 5 custom rules its plan allows.">
Remove a rule in the dashboard, or turn off another hostname's bot rules first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="… already uses all 10 Transform Rules its plan allows.">
Remove header rules from another hostname, or a Transform Rule in the dashboard.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="…'s plan has room for no other rate limit">
Teitunnel's existing rate limit on the domain is for other hostnames with another limit.
Use the same limit, or remove it from those hostnames first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Cloudflare doesn't let rules change … on requests.">
Headers such as `Host`, `X-Forwarded-For`, `X-Real-IP` and `Accept-Encoding` can't be
changed by Transform Rules. `Cookie` can only be removed from requests, not set.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The secret is no longer kept">
Teitunnel keeps a new secret for 10 minutes and never saves it. Rotate the token to get a
new one.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The account already has 50 service tokens, the most Cloudflare allows.">
Revoke one first, in Teitunnel or in the Zero Trust dashboard.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="… wasn't made by Teitunnel, so it's left alone.">
Manage that token in the Cloudflare dashboard.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
