---
title: Offline page
description: "Show your own page instead of Cloudflare's error 1033 when the computer serving a route is off or asleep, with a small Worker on your own Cloudflare account."
---

When the computer that serves a route is off, asleep or offline, Cloudflare can't reach
its tunnel and shows visitors **error 1033**, a Cloudflare error page that looks like the
site is broken. An **offline page** replaces it with a page of your own ("Back soon"),
served by a small Worker on your Cloudflare account, so it works while your computer is
off.

Use it for routes people visit when you're not watching: a portfolio or side project on a
laptop, a home server that restarts, a demo you send before a meeting. For a static site
that must stay fully online, publish a [Snapshot](/docs/guides/snapshots) instead.

## Turn it on in the app

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the route in **Routes**. Under **While This Computer Is Off** ("Workers on your
Cloudflare account keep answering when this computer is off or asleep."), choose
**Turn On…** next to **Offline page**.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Offline Page for app.teispace.com**, give it a **Title** (up to 120 characters) and a
**Message** (up to 1,000 characters; each line becomes a paragraph).
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally turn on **Also when the app doesn't answer**: it shows the page too when the
tunnel is up but the local service returns 502 or 504, for example because the app on this
computer crashed or wasn't started.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Review**, read the plan, and choose **Apply**. The plan warns about the cost
(see [Limits and costs](#limits-and-costs)). The route then shows **Edit…** and **Remove**
for the page.
</Step>
</Steps>

## From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel offline app.teispace.com --title "Back soon" --message "We're updating the demo. Try again in an hour."
teitunnel offline app.teispace.com --when-app-down      # also for 502/504 from the app
teitunnel offline app.teispace.com                      # what it has now
teitunnel offline app.teispace.com --off                # remove it
```

`--title` turns the page on; `--title`, `--message` and `--when-app-down` change only what
you pass and keep the rest. Without options, it prints the current title, message and
setting. `-a, --account` chooses the account and `-y, --yes` applies without asking.

## What happens on Cloudflare

The plan ("Show an offline page on app.teispace.com") has two steps:

1. **Upload the offline page Worker for app.teispace.com.** It's named `tt-offline-` and a
   short hash of the hostname.
2. **Run it for `app.teispace.com/*`** "(fails open past the free daily requests)": a
   Worker route on the hostname.

Removing it stops running the Worker for the pattern, then deletes the Worker. Undo in
[Activity](/docs/concepts/changes) reverses either change; a deleted Worker is restored from
its recorded settings. Removing a hostname's last route removes its offline page too.

Teitunnel only changes Workers and Worker routes it created. If another Worker already runs
on the same pattern, the plan stops with "Another Worker (…) already runs for
app.teispace.com/*. Teitunnel doesn't change Workers it didn't add." and changes nothing.

## How it works

The Worker passes every request on to your tunnel unchanged, without buffering bodies;
uploads, streaming and WebSockets work as before, and a working response is never changed.
Only when Cloudflare answers **530** (error 1033: no connector for the tunnel), or 502/504
with **Also when the app doesn't answer**, does it answer instead:

- **Pages** (a browser navigation): your page, with status **503**, `Retry-After: 60`,
  `Cache-Control: no-store` and `noindex`, so browsers and search engines treat it as
  temporary and never cache or index it. It follows the visitor's light or dark setting,
  loads nothing from elsewhere, and your text is always shown as text.
- **Everything else** (API calls, images, scripts): a short `503` JSON answer
  `{"error": "offline", "message": "<title>"}`.

## Limits and costs

| | Cloudflare free plan |
|---|---|
| Requests that run the Worker | Every request to the hostname, even while this computer is on |
| Free Worker requests | 100,000 a day for the whole account, reset at 00:00 UTC |
| Past the limit | The route **fails open**: the site keeps working without the Worker |

The Worker route is created to fail open (verified on a live Free-plan account on
2026-09-25: the dashboard shows "Request limit failure mode: Fail open (proceed)"). Once the
day's free requests are used up, requests go straight to your tunnel as if there were no
Worker; only the offline page is missing until 00:00 UTC. Your site never goes down because
of the limit. That's why the page is off by default and chosen per route, and why the plan
warns: "Every request to app.teispace.com/* runs a Worker and counts against your
account's 100,000 free Worker requests a day. Past that, the site keeps working without
it."

## Permissions

**Account · Workers Scripts · Edit** and **Zone · Workers Routes · Edit** on the route's
domain. Tokens created from Teitunnel's link and OAuth sign-in include both. If one is
missing, Teitunnel shows what to add and continues when you're back.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="… has no route through Cloudflare. Add a route for it first.">
An offline page sits in front of a route. Add the route first (a Quick Share on
trycloudflare.com can't have one).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Another Worker (…) already runs for …/*.">
A Worker you or someone else added in the dashboard already has a route on this hostname.
Remove that Worker route in the Cloudflare dashboard if you don't need it, then try again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Visitors still see error 1033">
The day's free Worker requests may be used up (the page is back at 00:00 UTC), or the
Worker route was removed in the dashboard: check `teitunnel offline app.teispace.com`, and
turn the page off and on again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Give the page a title of at most 120 characters.">
Shorten the title. The message can hold up to 1,000 characters.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
