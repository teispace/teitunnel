A **share** makes something on this computer public for a while: a dev server for a
colleague, a webhook endpoint for a provider, a built site for a review. There are two kinds:

| | Quick Share | Share on your domain |
|---|---|---|
| Address | Random: `https://quiet-river-1234.trycloudflare.com` | One you choose: `https://demo.teispace.com` |
| Needs | Nothing: no account, no domain | A connected Cloudflare account with a domain |
| What it is in Cloudflare | A throwaway Quick Tunnel | A temporary [route](/docs/concepts/routes-and-tunnels) on this computer's tunnel, with its DNS record |
| Pause and schedules | Pause all from the [menu bar](/docs/guides/menu-bar) | Pause, resume and schedules per share |
| Limits | 200 requests in flight, no server-sent events, no uptime guarantee | Your domain's plan |
| Ends | When you stop it, it times out, or Teitunnel quits | The same; its route and DNS record are then removed |

Both go through Teitunnel's [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector) by default, so you see each
request, and both run the [exposure check](/docs/guides/exposure-check) first. For something
that should stay up, add a [route](/docs/getting-started/first-route) instead; to keep a site
online while this computer sleeps, publish a [Snapshot](/docs/guides/snapshots).

## Share in the app

<Screenshot name="quick-share" alt="Quick Share: a live share with its trycloudflare.com URL, request count and log" />

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Choose what to share

    Open **Quick Share** in the sidebar (or **File ▸ New Quick Share…**). Type a port or an
    address in **Port or address** (`3000`, `localhost:5173`, `https://localhost:8443`), or
    pick one of the services **Running on this Mac**: Teitunnel lists dev servers, Docker
    containers and other local services with their project names. To share a folder, drop it
    on the window or choose **Share a Folder…** (see [below](#share-a-folder)).
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose the address

    Under **Address**, keep **Random address** for a Quick Share, or pick **On teispace.com**
    (one entry per domain of the account) and type a **Subdomain**, such as `demo`.
    Teitunnel suggests names from the service's project (the one you used last time first)
    and shows what a name with [placeholders](#names-from-your-project-and-branch) becomes.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Choose when to stop

    **When to stop**: **Keep running**, **Stop after 15 min**, **Stop after 1 hour** or
    **Stop after 8 hours**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Advanced options (optional)

    Under **Advanced**:

    - **Host header**: **Automatic** sends the service's own address to dev servers that only
      answer it (Vite, webpack, Angular); **As the browser sends it** passes the visitor's;
      **Custom** sends the value you type, such as `localhost:5173`.
    - **Inspect requests**: on by default. Off sends requests straight to the service: faster
      for large downloads, but no request list, pausing or protection.
    - For folders: file listings and single-page-app mode.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    ### Share

    Choose **Share**. The exposure check runs first; if it finds something, you choose
    **Share Anyway** or **Cancel**. The share's card shows *Getting a URL…*, then **Live**
    with the address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each running share has a card with its address and status, **Open in browser**, the copy
button, **Show QR code** (for opening it on a phone), request and error counts, *Stops in …*,
its **Log**, **Stop Sharing**, and, for shares on your domain, **Pause** and **Schedule**.
**Publish a Snapshot** turns the shared site into a static copy that stays online.

Shares started from a terminal appear on the same page under **From a terminal**, and shares on
your domain say *Removed when you stop it or quit Teitunnel* or *Started from the command
line*.

## Share from a terminal

```sh
teitunnel share 3000                            # a Quick Share of localhost:3000
teitunnel share 3000 --for 2h                   # stops by itself after 2 hours
teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.teispace.com     # on your domain, until Ctrl-C
teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.teispace.com --allow team@teispace.com   # behind a login
teitunnel share ./dist                          # a folder
teitunnel shares                                # every share, wherever it runs
teitunnel shares --stop demo.teispace.com
```

```text
$ teitunnel share 3000
https://quiet-river-1234.trycloudflare.com

█▀▀▀▀▀█ ▄▀▄ ▀▄█ █▀▀▀▀▀█
…
http://localhost:3000 is public at https://quiet-river-1234.trycloudflare.com for anyone with the link. Press Ctrl-C to stop.
14:02:11  GET     200    18 ms    2.1 KB  quiet-river-1234.trycloudflare.com/  3c9e1f0a
```

The address alone goes to standard output (so `teitunnel share 3000 | head -1` works in
scripts; `--json` prints `{"url": …, "hostname": …}` instead), and everything else to
standard error. A QR code is printed when the output is a terminal (`--no-qr` leaves it out),
and a line per request unless you pass `--quiet`.

**When the app is running**, `teitunnel share` hands a Quick Share to the app (which may ask
you to allow it) and returns; the share keeps running there. `--here` keeps it in the
terminal instead, and `--app` fails if the app isn't running. A share on your domain, a
folder, or one with `--no-inspect`, `--idle` or `--watch` always runs in the terminal, for as
long as the command runs.

| Flag | What it does |
|---|---|
| `--for DURATION` | Stop after `30m`, `2h`, `90s`… |
| `--on [HOSTNAME]` | Share on your domain instead of a random address. Alone, reuses the name last used in this folder. |
| `--account`, `-a` | With `--on`: the account, when several are connected. |
| `--allow EMAIL\|@DOMAIN` | With `--on`: require a [login](/docs/guides/require-login) (repeatable). |
| `--schedule "DAYS HH:MM-HH:MM"`, `--tz ZONE` | With `--on`: serve only during these hours ([schedules](#on-a-schedule)). |
| `--host-header HOST` / `--no-host-header` | Send this Host header to the service, or pass the visitor's through unchanged. |
| `--listing`, `--spa` | With a folder: list files, or serve `index.html` for unknown paths. |
| `--no-inspect` | Don't send requests through the inspector. |
| `--idle DURATION` | Stop after this long without a request. |
| `--watch PATH` | Say so when a request hits this path, such as `/webhooks/*` (repeatable). |
| `--strict` | Don't share when the exposure check finds something. |
| `--json`, `--quiet`, `--no-qr` | Output for scripts, no request lines, no QR code. |
| `--here`, `--app` | Where the share runs (see above). |
| `--mcp`, `--ai` | Share a local MCP server or AI server behind a bearer token; see [the inspector](/docs/guides/inspector#put-an-mcp-server-or-a-local-ai-server-online). |

## Shares on your domain

A share on your domain is a **temporary route**: Teitunnel plans and applies it like any
other change (tunnel configuration, then the DNS record, then a check), and removes the route
and its record when the share ends. It's served by this computer's tunnel, so the app's
connector (or the command's, in a terminal) must be running.

- **It never takes over someone else's name.** A hostname with a DNS record Teitunnel didn't
  create is refused, and so is one someone on your team
  [reserved](/docs/concepts/dns-ownership#reservations) or routes on another machine.
- **Nothing is left behind.** The share is recorded before its route is applied. If the app
  or a terminal running a share quits or crashes, the app removes those routes within 30
  seconds, or at its next launch.
- **Logins:** `--allow` puts a Cloudflare Access login in front of it, like a route's.

### Names from your project and branch

A hostname may contain `{project}`, `{branch}` and `{user}`, the same placeholders as
[project files](/docs/guides/project-file):

```sh
teitunnel share 3000 --on {branch}.dev.teispace.com     # login-fix.dev.teispace.com
teitunnel share 3000 --on {project}-{user}.teispace.com # shop-<your user name>.teispace.com
teitunnel share 3000 --on                               # the name used in this folder last time
```

`{project}` is the name in the folder's `package.json`, `Cargo.toml`, `pyproject.toml`,
`composer.json` or `go.mod` (else the folder's name), `{branch}` is read from the git
repository the folder is in (no git process is started), and `{user}` is your user name. Every
value becomes a valid DNS label, and the command says what the name became: *\{branch\}.dev.teispace.com
is login-fix.dev.teispace.com here.* The name you used is remembered for the folder.

## Pause and resume

Pausing keeps the address: the route and its DNS record stay, and visitors get a small
"paused" page (HTTP 503 with `Retry-After`) until you resume. Resume, and the same address
serves your service again at once, with no DNS change to wait for.

- **App:** **Pause** on the share's card. A **Paused** badge shows while it lasts;
  **Resume** ends it.
- **Terminal:** `teitunnel shares --pause demo.teispace.com`, then
  `teitunnel shares --resume demo.teispace.com`.
- **Quick Shares:** **Pause All Shares** in the [menu bar](/docs/guides/menu-bar) pauses
  every inspected Quick Share.
- **AI agents:** `pause_share` and `resume_share`, with your approval.

The paused page is shown by Teitunnel's inspector in the process that serves the share: the
app, the terminal running `teitunnel share --on`, or `teitunnel up`/`serve`. A share that
isn't inspected is pointed at the inspector first (the same reviewed change as
**Inspect this route**) and pointed back when you resume.

You can pause one of this computer's **permanent routes** the same way, while the app,
`teitunnel up` or `teitunnel serve` runs to show the page. When none of them runs, Teitunnel
says so rather than pretending.

## On a schedule

A schedule makes a share on your domain, or a route, available only during set hours on set
days, and paused the rest of the time: a demo that's only up during office hours, a staging
site that's closed at night.

- **App:** **Schedule** on the share's card. Choose the **Days**, the hours **From** and
  **to**, and **Save**. The card then shows *On a schedule: …* with the days and hours, and
  when it next changes. **Remove Schedule** takes it off.
- **Terminal:**

  ```sh
  teitunnel schedule demo.teispace.com mon-fri 09:00-18:00
  teitunnel schedule demo.teispace.com weekends 10:00-16:00 --tz Europe/Berlin
  teitunnel schedule demo.teispace.com            # show it
  teitunnel schedule demo.teispace.com --off      # remove it; the share stays as it is
  teitunnel schedules                             # every schedule
  teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.teispace.com --schedule "mon-fri 09:00-18:00"
  ```

  ```text
  $ teitunnel schedules
  https://demo.teispace.com	mon,tue,wed,thu,fri 09:00-18:00 (local time)	on, pauses at Wed 18:00
  ```

- **AI agents:** `schedule_share`.

Days are written like `mon-fri`, `sat,sun`, `weekdays`, `weekends` or `daily`, and times in
24-hour `HH:MM`. Times are wall-clock times in your computer's time zone, or in the one you
name with `--tz`, so daylight saving time keeps 09:00 at 09:00. A window that ends before it
starts runs past midnight (`fri 22:00-02:00`). Pausing or resuming by hand holds until the
schedule's next change.

Whoever serves the share applies its schedule within 30 seconds: the app, `teitunnel up`,
`teitunnel serve`, the terminal running the share, or `teitunnel mcp`. For this computer's
routes, when none of them runs, routes are served normally.

## Share a folder

Share a folder of files (a built site, docs, a design export) without running a server:

- **App:** drop a folder on the Quick Share window, or choose **Share a Folder…**. Under
  **Advanced**, turn on **List the files of folders without an index page**, or
  **Single-page app (every page opens index.html)**. **Share a service instead** goes back.
- **Terminal:** `teitunnel share ./dist` (a random address) or
  `teitunnel share ./dist --on docs.teispace.com`, with `--listing` and `--spa`.
- **AI agents:** `share_folder`.

Teitunnel's inspector serves the files, so a folder share can't turn inspection off. *Hidden
files, .env files, keys, .git and node_modules are never shared*, nothing outside the folder is
ever served (links that lead out of it are refused), and sharing the whole disk or your home
folder is refused: *… holds far more than a site. Share the folder with the files you want
people to see, like dist or public.*

## Stop after a while, or when idle

- **After a fixed time:** **When to stop** in the app, `--for 2h` in the terminal. The card
  shows *Stops in …*.
- **When nobody uses it:** **Settings ▸ Inspector ▸ Stop idle shares** stops any share after
  15 minutes to 8 hours without a request (off by default). Per share, it's in the share's
  **Inspection Settings… ▸ More**, or `--idle 30m` in the terminal. You get a notification:
  *demo.teispace.com had no requests for 30 minutes, so it stopped.*

## Inspection on or off

Shares go through the inspector unless you turn it off. Turning it off or on for a running
Quick Share restarts it, so it gets a new address; the app asks first (*The share restarts,
so it gets a new address.*). The default for new Quick Shares is **Settings ▸ Inspector ▸
Inspect Quick Shares**.

Without the inspector, a share has no request list, no paused page, no idle stop and no
[protection on this computer](/docs/guides/inspector#per-share-or-route). With it, the
inspector adds a few milliseconds per request, and keeps event streams alive past
Cloudflare's 100-second limit.

## Troubleshooting

- **"Cloudflare didn't provide a URL in time. Check your internet connection and try
  again."** A Quick Share waits 20 seconds for trycloudflare.com. It's sometimes busy; try
  again, or share on your own domain.
- **"This Quick Share hit its limit of 200 requests at once. Share on your own domain for
  more."** Quick Tunnels carry at most 200 requests in flight.
- **The page loads but a dev server answers "Invalid Host header" or "Blocked request".**
  Set **Host header** to **Automatic** or **Custom** (`--host-header localhost:5173`).
  Teitunnel's check usually spots it and offers the fix.
- **"The terminal that shared demo.teispace.com has stopped, so there's nothing to pause."**
  The share ended with its terminal. Share it again.
- **"demo.teispace.com runs in a terminal without Teitunnel's inspector (--no-inspect), which
  is what shows the paused page."** Stop it and share it again without `--no-inspect`.
- **"Pausing demo.teispace.com needs Teitunnel running on this computer to show the paused
  page: open the app, or run teitunnel up or teitunnel serve."**
- **"No name was used for a share from this folder yet; give one: --on demo.teispace.com (or
  --on \{branch\}.dev.teispace.com)."** `--on` alone only works after you've shared from this
  folder once.
- **"\{branch\} has no value here (the branch comes from the git repository the share starts
  in)."** Run the command inside the repository, or use another placeholder.
- **"Too many Quick Shares are running."** Stop some; each share runs its own cloudflared.
