---
title: Webhook inbox
description: "Keep webhooks (Stripe, GitHub, anything) while your computer is off, and deliver them in order when it's back, with a Worker and a D1 database on your own Cloudflare account."
---

Webhook senders give up after a few retries, so webhooks sent while your laptop sleeps are
often lost, and you only find out when a payment or a deploy never showed up in your app. A
**webhook inbox** keeps them on your own Cloudflare account and delivers them to your app,
in order, once this computer is back.

Use it on a route that receives webhooks you can't afford to miss: Stripe events in a
local billing app, GitHub events for a bot you run on a home server, form submissions to a
side project. To develop and debug webhooks, start with the
[webhooks tutorial](/docs/tutorials/webhooks).

## Add one in the app

<Screenshot name="offline-inbox" alt="A route in Routes: While This Computer Is Off, with an offline page and a webhook inbox holding three webhooks" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the route in **Routes**. Under **While This Computer Is Off**, choose **Add Webhook
Inbox…**.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Webhook Inbox for hooks.teispace.com**, set:

- **Path:** webhooks to this path and below, for example `/webhooks/` or `/api/stripe`. It
  must start with `/` and have no `*`, `?` or spaces.
- **Keep up to** 500 **webhooks for** 7 **days**: 1 to 1,000 webhooks, kept 1 to 30 days.
- **Keep only signed webhooks:** **Keep every webhook**, **GitHub**, **Stripe** or
  **Standard Webhooks (Svix, Clerk, Resend…)**. See [below](#only-keep-signed-webhooks).
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Review**, read the plan, and choose **Apply**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The route then lists the inbox ("Webhooks to /webhooks/") with each webhook, when it
arrived, and whether it's **Waiting** or **Delivered** (with the status your app answered).
**Deliver Now** delivers waiting ones at once.

## From the terminal

```sh
teitunnel inbox add hooks.teispace.com /webhooks/ --max 500 --days 7
teitunnel inbox ls                                   # offline pages and inboxes
teitunnel inbox items hooks.teispace.com /webhooks/  # recent webhooks, arrived and delivered
teitunnel inbox deliver                              # deliver waiting webhooks now
teitunnel inbox rm hooks.teispace.com /webhooks/
```

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| `inbox add HOSTNAME PATH` | Keep webhooks to a path while this computer is off. `--max` (default 500, up to 1,000), `--days` (default 7, up to 30), `--verify github\|stripe\|standard`. |
| `inbox rm HOSTNAME PATH` | Remove an inbox. Webhooks still waiting stay in the database until their retention ends. |
| `inbox ls` | Offline pages and inboxes on this computer's records. `--json`. |
| `inbox items HOSTNAME PATH` | An inbox's recent webhooks. `--json`. |
| `inbox secret HOSTNAME PROVIDER` | Save a signing secret in the keychain. |
| `inbox deliver` | Deliver waiting webhooks now. |

`add` and `rm` show the plan first; `-a, --account` chooses the account and `-y, --yes`
applies without asking.

## What happens to a webhook

Only `POST`, `PUT`, `PATCH` and `DELETE` requests are kept. Anything else, such as a
sender's `GET` verification handshake, always goes straight through.

| Situation | What the inbox does | The sender gets |
|---|---|---|
| This computer is on, nothing waiting | Passes it on to your app, unchanged | Your app's answer |
| This computer is off (Cloudflare can't reach the tunnel, 530) | Stores it | `202 Accepted` |
| The app doesn't answer (cloudflared can't reach it, 502) | Stores it | `202 Accepted` |
| Earlier webhooks are still waiting | Stores it behind them, so order is kept | `202 Accepted` |
| The inbox is full | Doesn't store it | `503` with `Retry-After`, so the sender retries later |
| Body larger than 512 KB | Passes it through; while offline it's refused | `413` while offline |
| Signature required and missing or wrong | Doesn't store it | `401` while offline |

Cookies, `cf-*` headers and forwarding headers are dropped before a webhook is stored.
Webhooks older than the retention are deleted, delivered or not.

## Delivery

While the app runs, it delivers waiting webhooks every 30 seconds, oldest first. Each goes
from this computer directly to the route's own local service (the one the route points
at), with its original method, path, headers and body, plus:

- `X-Teitunnel-Inbox`: the webhook's id in the inbox.
- `X-Teitunnel-Received-At`: when it arrived at Cloudflare.

An answer below 500 counts as delivered. A 5xx or no answer stops the round, so later
webhooks never overtake it, and it's tried again next time. Redirects aren't followed.
While the route is [inspected](/docs/guides/inspector), deliveries go through the inspector
and show there with their headers.

<Callout type="warn" title="On a server">
`teitunnel up` and `teitunnel serve` don't deliver inboxes by themselves. On a server, run
`teitunnel inbox deliver` on a timer (a systemd timer or cron job), or keep the app
running.
</Callout>

### Signatures and replays
The body and headers are delivered unchanged, so signatures still verify. But senders that
sign a timestamp (Stripe, Standard Webhooks) are usually checked with a 5-minute
tolerance, and a webhook delivered hours later is older than that. Give your app's check a
longer tolerance, or rely on the inbox's own check (below), which runs when the webhook
arrives.

## Only keep signed webhooks

An inbox can keep only webhooks with a valid signature, so nobody else can fill it with
junk while you're away. It checks GitHub (`X-Hub-Signature-256`), Stripe
(`Stripe-Signature`) and Standard Webhooks (Svix, Clerk, Resend and others; timestamps within
5 minutes).

Save the signing secret for the hostname once. The [inspector](/docs/guides/inspector)
uses the same secret to check signatures. In the app, choose the sender under **Keep only
signed webhooks**, paste the secret and click **Save Secret** (**Review** waits for it). In
a terminal:

```sh
teitunnel inbox secret hooks.teispace.com stripe     # reads it from standard input
TEITUNNEL_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_… teitunnel inbox secret hooks.teispace.com stripe
teitunnel inbox add hooks.teispace.com /webhooks/stripe --verify stripe
```

The secret is never read from the command line. It's kept in your system's keychain, and
Teitunnel sends it to Cloudflare only as a secret of the inbox's Worker: it's never shown
again, and never in the plan or Activity.

## What happens on Cloudflare

The plan ("Keep webhooks to hooks.teispace.com/webhooks/ while this computer is off"):

1. **Create the D1 database `teitunnel-data`** for comments and webhooks, only the first
   time. One database per account holds every inbox and
   [Snapshot comments](/docs/guides/comments).
2. **Upload the webhook inbox Worker** (named `tt-inbox-` and a short hash).
3. **Run it for `hooks.teispace.com/webhooks/*`** "(fails open past the free daily
   requests)".

Removing an inbox stops the Worker route and deletes the Worker. Undo in Activity reverses
either change. Undoing the removal of a verifying inbox brings the Worker back without its
secret: add the inbox again to restore signature checks. Removing a hostname's last route
removes its inboxes too.

The inbox and an [offline page](/docs/guides/offline-page) can share a hostname: the more
specific Worker route (the inbox's path) wins for its path. Teitunnel never takes over a
pattern another Worker already runs on.

## Limits and costs

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| Webhooks waiting per inbox | 1 to 1,000 (500 by default) |
| Retention | 1 to 30 days (7 by default) |
| Body size kept | 512 KB |
| Worker requests | Every request to the inbox's path; 100,000 free a day per account, then the route fails open |
| D1 (free) | 5 million rows read and 100,000 written a day; 5 GB |

Past the free Worker requests, the route fails open: webhooks go straight to your tunnel as
if there were no inbox, and are only kept again after 00:00 UTC.

## Permissions

**Account · Workers Scripts · Edit**, **Zone · Workers Routes · Edit** on the route's domain,
and **Account · D1 · Edit**. If one is missing, Teitunnel shows what to add and continues
when you're back.

## Troubleshooting

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="The webhook path must start with / and have no *, ? or spaces, e.g. /webhooks/.">
Use a plain path prefix. `/webhooks/` covers `/webhooks/stripe` and everything below it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="An inbox holds 1 to 1000 webhooks for 1 to 30 days.">
Choose values inside those ranges.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Verifying signatures needs the webhook's signing secret.">
Save it first: in the inbox sheet (**Save Secret**) or with
`teitunnel inbox secret <hostname> <provider>`, then add the inbox.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="There's nothing of Teitunnel's to remove at …">
No inbox of Teitunnel's is on that hostname and path. Check `teitunnel inbox ls`.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Waiting: … stays on a webhook">
Your app answered 5xx or didn't answer. Start it, check its logs, then **Deliver Now**.
Later webhooks wait behind this one, so order is kept.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your app rejects delivered webhooks as too old">
The sender's timestamp is checked with a short tolerance. Increase it for requests that
carry `X-Teitunnel-Inbox`, and let the inbox verify signatures on arrival.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
