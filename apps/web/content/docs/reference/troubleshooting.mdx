Start with the **Doctor** (⌘7 / Ctrl+7, or `teitunnel doctor`): it checks your routes,
DNS, connectors, logins and local domains, and most problems below come with a one-click
fix there. Then **Test** on a route checks it end to end through Cloudflare. If it still
doesn't work, find what you see below.

## In the browser

### Error 1033

*Cloudflare Tunnel error: Cloudflare is currently unable to resolve it.* The hostname points
at a tunnel with **no connector running**.

- The app is closed and the route isn't Always-on: open Teitunnel, or turn on **Keep running
  when Teitunnel quits** ([run modes](/docs/concepts/run-modes)).
- The connector can't reach Cloudflare: see [connections keep dropping](#connections-keep-dropping).
- The record points at a tunnel that was deleted: the Doctor reports it (`dns.orphan_*`) and
  offers to delete or repoint it.

To show visitors your own page instead while your computer is off, add an
[offline page](/docs/guides/offline-page).

### 502 Bad Gateway

The tunnel is up, but cloudflared **couldn't get an answer from your service**.

- **Nothing listening on that port.** Start your app, or fix the port in the route. The Doctor
  says *Nothing is listening on port 8000* (`origin.not_listening`).
- **`localhost` resolves differently.** Some servers listen only on `127.0.0.1` (IPv4) or only
  on `::1` (IPv6). Point the route at the exact address, such as `http://127.0.0.1:3000`, or
  make your server listen on both.
- **HTTP versus HTTPS.** A route to `http://…` on a port that speaks HTTPS (or the reverse)
  fails. Match the scheme; for a self-signed HTTPS origin, turn on **No TLS verify**.
- **In Docker**, `localhost` is the container itself; see
  [Docker Compose](/docs/tutorials/docker-compose#things-that-trip-people-up).
- **A route left pointing at the inspector.** If an app or command that was
  [inspecting](/docs/guides/inspector) the route stopped without restoring it, the Doctor
  reports `inspect.orphan`; **Restore the Route** fixes it.

The connector's log (**Tunnels ▸ Logs**, or the route's **Logs**) shows cloudflared's exact
error for each failed request.

### Error 530 or 1016

*Origin DNS error.* Cloudflare couldn't resolve where to send the request, usually because the
DNS record points at a tunnel ID that no longer exists or a mistyped target. Run the Doctor:
*A record points at a tunnel that no longer exists* offers to delete or reroute it.

Right after a route or a share on your domain is added, Cloudflare takes up to about half a
minute to connect the new record to the tunnel and answers 1016 until then (measured
2026-09-25). Teitunnel waits that out: the share says *Cloudflare is still connecting this
address* and checks again by itself. After you stop a share, a browser that visited it may
show 1016 for a few more minutes from its DNS cache, although the record is already gone.

### The site can't be reached (DNS)

`DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN` or *server IP address could not be found*:

- The domain isn't active on Cloudflare yet: **Domains** shows *Waiting for nameservers*
  until your registrar uses Cloudflare's nameservers.
- The record is missing: the Doctor's `dns.missing` adds it.
- Your computer cached the old answer: wait a few minutes, or flush your DNS cache.
- A brand-new **Quick Share** address looked up too early can be cached as missing for up to
  30 minutes by your resolver. Try it on another network or device, or start a new share.

### SSL handshake failed, or ERR_SSL_VERSION_OR_CIPHER_MISMATCH

Usually a **multi-level subdomain** such as `app.dev.teispace.com`. Cloudflare's free
Universal SSL certificate covers `teispace.com` and one level below (`*.teispace.com`) only.
Use a single-level name (`app-dev.teispace.com`), or order an Advanced Certificate or Total
TLS for the deeper level in the Cloudflare dashboard.

Right after adding a new domain, Cloudflare can take a little while to issue its certificate.

### Too many redirects

`ERR_TOO_MANY_REDIRECTS`: your app redirects HTTP to HTTPS, but cloudflared talks to it over
HTTP, so it redirects forever. Either let the app trust the proxy header
`X-Forwarded-Proto: https` (Express `trust proxy`, Django `SECURE_PROXY_SSL_HEADER`, Rails
`config.assume_ssl`, WordPress `$_SERVER['HTTPS']` behind a proxy), or point the route at
the app's HTTPS port.

Also check the domain's **SSL/TLS encryption mode** in Cloudflare isn't set to force a
different scheme through a Page Rule or redirect rule you forgot.

### Blocked request, or Invalid Host header

Vite, webpack, Angular, Next.js, Django and Rails refuse hostnames they don't know.
Teitunnel recognises their answer when a share or route goes live and offers the fix in
place. See [dev servers](/docs/tutorials/dev-servers): send the dev server's own Host header,
or allow the hostname in the project.

### 524 timeout, 413 too large, or 429 on a Quick Share

- **524** means your service took more than 100 seconds to answer. Make long jobs
  asynchronous, or stream progress.
- **413** means the upload was over 100 MB (Free and Pro plans). Upload in chunks.
- **429** on a Quick Share means 200 requests were in flight at once, a Quick Share's limit.
  Share on your own domain for more.

### A login loop, or 403 after signing in

On a route that [requires a login](/docs/guides/require-login): make sure the address you
sign in with is on the list (an exact address, or `@teispace.com` for a whole domain). Test
in a private window to rule out an old session. Routes with a regular-expression path can't
have a login; use one route per path.

## Quick Shares

### A Quick Share serves the wrong thing, or won't start

cloudflared reads `~/.cloudflared/config.yml` when it finds one, and that file's `ingress`
rules win over the address you share. Teitunnel always starts Quick Shares with an empty
config file of its own, so a leftover file can't get in the way; if you run
`cloudflared tunnel --url` yourself, move the file aside first.

### Streaming, MCP servers or live reload don't work on a Quick Share

Quick Shares (`trycloudflare.com`) don't carry **Server-Sent Events**, so event streams,
MCP servers over SSE and some live-reload setups hang. They also allow 200 requests in
flight and have no uptime guarantee. Share on your own domain instead (**Address ▸ On**
*your domain*, or `teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.teispace.com`); `teitunnel share --mcp`
needs `--on` for this reason. See [Limits and costs](/docs/reference/limits#quick-shares).

## Accounts and permissions

### Invalid access token (9109)

Cloudflare answers `Invalid access token` (code 9109) when the access token of a
**Sign in with Cloudflare** account is no longer valid. In test builds before 0.2.0, the
app, the `teitunnel` command and an AI agent's MCP server each refreshed the sign-in on
their own; since Cloudflare issues a new refresh token each time, one of them could cancel
the others' tokens until the app restarted.

0.2.0 fixes this: all of them share one access token through the keychain. If you still
see it:

1. Make sure you run 0.2.0 or later, then quit and reopen Teitunnel, and restart any AI
   tool that runs `teitunnel mcp`.
2. If it persists, the sign-in was revoked in Cloudflare: **Disconnect** the account in
   **Settings ▸ Accounts** and connect it again.

With an **API token**, an invalid-token error means the token was deleted, expired or
rolled in the dashboard: connect the account again with a working token.

### Access is not enabled, or "Set up Cloudflare Zero Trust"

Logins and service tokens use Cloudflare Zero Trust (Access). On an account that has never
used it, Cloudflare answers that Access isn't enabled, and Teitunnel shows
**Set up Cloudflare Zero Trust** (or *Requiring a login needs Cloudflare Zero Trust, which
isn't set up for this account*). It's not a permission problem, and a new token won't help:

1. **Open Zero Trust** and choose a team name.
2. Pick the **Free** plan (up to 50 users), then come back to Teitunnel.

It checks again when you switch back and carries on. In **Settings ▸ Accounts ▸
Permissions** the row reads *Needs Cloudflare Zero Trust, which isn't set up yet* until then.

### "The token needs one more permission"

A feature needs a permission the account's credential doesn't have. The card lists each
one by its dashboard name. For an API token: **Open API Tokens**, **Edit** the token, add
them, **Continue to Summary**, **Update Token**, and switch back; Teitunnel checks again
by itself. For a sign-in, connect the account with an API token that has them. The
Doctor's `auth.missing_scope` means the token can't manage routes at all (it lacks
*Cloudflare Tunnel · Edit* or *DNS · Edit*). Every permission and what needs it is in
[Accounts and permissions](/docs/guides/accounts#every-permission-and-what-needs-it).

### Rate limiting is refused on a Free domain

*… is on Cloudflare's Free plan, whose rate limit can't be limited to one hostname.* On
Free, a rate limiting rule can only match paths, so it would apply to the whole domain.
Teitunnel only adds rules for one hostname, so rate limiting needs Pro or higher. Bot and AI
crawler rules and header rules work on Free. See [Edge protection](/docs/guides/protection#your-plans-limits).

### The keychain couldn't be used

Unlock your keychain and try again. On Linux, install and unlock a Secret Service provider
(GNOME Keyring or KWallet). On a server without one, use `CLOUDFLARE_API_TOKEN`; see
[Servers and containers](/docs/guides/servers#the-api-token).

## Workers and D1 limits

### The offline page or webhook inbox stopped for the rest of the day

On the Free plan, Workers run for **100,000 requests a day per account** (reset at 00:00
UTC). The [offline page](/docs/guides/offline-page) and [webhook inbox](/docs/guides/webhook-inbox)
run a Worker for every request to their hostname, even when your computer is on. Teitunnel
creates their Worker routes to **fail open**: past the limit, requests go straight to your
tunnel as if no Worker were there. Your site keeps working; only the offline page (or
keeping webhooks while you're offline) is lost until the next day. Snapshots with a
password or comments count the same way. For more, remove them from busy hostnames, or move to
Workers Paid.

### The webhook inbox answers 503

The inbox keeps at most **1,000 webhooks** of up to 512 KB each. When it's full it answers
503 with `Retry-After`, and senders retry later. Bring the computer back online so it
delivers what's waiting, or shorten the inbox's retention.

### Comments or the inbox fail with a D1 error

Snapshot comments and inboxes use one D1 database on your account, `teitunnel-data`. On the
Free plan D1 allows **5 million rows read and 100,000 rows written a day**, 500 MB per
database and 10 databases per account; past a daily limit, Cloudflare refuses queries until
it resets. If creating the database fails, check whether the account already has 10.

### A Snapshot is too big

Snapshots are limited to **20,000 files** per version and **25 MiB** per file on the Free
plan. Teitunnel checks this before uploading and says which files are over. Leave out large
media, or host it elsewhere. See [Snapshots](/docs/guides/snapshots#limits-and-costs).

## On your computer

### Connections keep dropping

- Some networks block **UDP**, which cloudflared prefers (QUIC on port 7844). It falls back
  to HTTP/2 over TCP by itself; the Doctor reports `net.udp_blocked`. Allow outbound port
  7844 (UDP and TCP) in strict firewalls.
- A wrong system **clock** breaks TLS: the Doctor reports `net.clock_skew`.
- Two machines running the **same tunnel** split its requests between them; the Doctor
  reports `tunnel.other_connectors`.

### A local domain shows a certificate warning, or doesn't open

- *Browsers don't trust your local domains yet* (`local.untrusted`): trust Teitunnel's
  certificate authority once, from the Doctor or **Local Domains**.
- *… doesn't reach this computer yet* (`local.resolver`): `.test` names need a one-time
  system entry that needs an administrator; Teitunnel shows the command. `.localhost` names
  work without it.
- *Local domains use port 8443 instead of 443* (`local.port`): another app has the usual
  port, so the address includes a port number. Quit that app and restart local domains.
- *… points at another computer* (`local.elsewhere`): your hosts file or DNS sends the
  name elsewhere.

See [Local domains](/docs/guides/local-domains).

### WARP users can't reach a private network

Usually **Split Tunnels** exclude the range, or the **Gateway proxy** is off. See
[private networks](/docs/guides/private-networks#share-a-private-network) and the Doctor's
`network.*` checks.

### Tunnels show no machines

From 2026-10-05, Cloudflare no longer lists a tunnel's connections with the tunnel. Teitunnel
0.2.0 reads them from Cloudflare's new endpoint; 0.1.x shows every tunnel as if nothing ran
it. [Update](/docs/getting-started/install#updates) to 0.2.0.
