The terms you'll meet in Teitunnel and its docs, in alphabetical order. Each links to the
page that explains it.

## A–C

**Access** (Cloudflare Access). Cloudflare's login service, part of Zero Trust. Teitunnel
uses it to put a login in front of a route, share or Snapshot, and for service tokens.
[Require a login](/docs/guides/require-login)

**Account.** A Cloudflare account connected to Teitunnel with Sign in with Cloudflare, an
API token or a cloudflared login. One sign-in or token can connect several.
[Accounts and permissions](/docs/guides/accounts)

**Activity.** The record of every change Teitunnel made to your Cloudflare account, and who
made it: you, a program or an AI agent.
[How changes are made](/docs/concepts/changes#activity)

**Always-on.** A run mode where this computer's connector runs as a system service, from
login or boot, even when Teitunnel is closed. The switch is
**Keep running when Teitunnel quits**. [Run modes](/docs/concepts/run-modes)

**API token.** A credential you create in the Cloudflare dashboard with chosen permissions.
One way to connect an account, and the way servers and CI use Teitunnel.
[Accounts and permissions](/docs/guides/accounts#connect-an-account)

**Apply.** Carrying out a reviewed plan on Cloudflare, step by step, rolling back if a step
fails. [How changes are made](/docs/concepts/changes)

**Backup.** An encrypted file with Teitunnel's setup, for moving to a new computer. It never
holds a token or password. [Move to a new computer](/docs/guides/move-computer)

**cloudflared.** Cloudflare's open-source connector program. Teitunnel installs, checks and
runs it for you. [What is Cloudflare Tunnel?](/docs/concepts/cloudflare-tunnel)

**Comments.** Notes reviewers pin to a page of a share or Snapshot, which you read and
answer in Teitunnel. [Comments](/docs/guides/comments)

**Connector.** A running `cloudflared` that holds a tunnel's connections to Cloudflare. A
route answers only while a connector for its tunnel runs somewhere.
[Routes and tunnels](/docs/concepts/routes-and-tunnels)

**Control connection.** The private local channel through which the `teitunnel` command,
editor extensions, launchers and `teitunnel://` links talk to the running app. Only your
user can open it, and changes ask you first.
[Links and integrations](/docs/guides/integrations#the-control-connection)

## D–I

**Doctor.** Teitunnel's checks for problems with cloudflared, accounts, DNS, connectors,
private networks and local domains, each with a fix or guidance.
[Doctor checks](/docs/reference/doctor)

**Domain.** A domain on your Cloudflare account (Cloudflare calls it a zone), with its
nameservers pointed at Cloudflare. Routes and shares on your domain live on one.
[Put a service on your domain](/docs/getting-started/first-route)

**Edge protection.** Cloudflare rules for one hostname, set up by Teitunnel: challenge or
block bots and AI crawlers, rate limit visitors, set or remove headers.
[Edge protection](/docs/guides/protection)

**Exposure check.** A quick local look at a service before it's shared, for `.env` files,
`.git`, debug pages and open admin tools. [Exposure check](/docs/guides/exposure-check)

**Front Worker.** A Cloudflare Worker Teitunnel puts in front of a route's hostname: the
offline page and the webhook inbox. It fails open, so the free daily limit can't take the
site down. [Offline page](/docs/guides/offline-page)

**Inbox** (webhook inbox). A front Worker that keeps webhooks sent while your computer is off
and delivers them in order when it's back. [Webhook inbox](/docs/guides/webhook-inbox)

**Inspector.** Teitunnel's view of every request to an inspected share or route, with
replay, edit and export, credentials masked. [Inspector](/docs/guides/inspector)

## L–P

**Load balancing.** Several computers serving one hostname, with Cloudflare sending each
visitor to a healthy one. A paid Cloudflare add-on. [Load balancing](/docs/guides/load-balancing)

**Local domain.** A name such as `shop.test`, `app.localhost` or `name.local` that opens a
local service over trusted HTTPS on your computer, with no Cloudflare account.
[Local domains](/docs/guides/local-domains)

**MCP** (Model Context Protocol). The standard AI agents use to call tools. Teitunnel's MCP
server lets agents share services, manage routes through plans you approve, run the Doctor
and read traffic. [AI agents](/docs/guides/ai-agents)

**Offline page.** Your own page, shown by a front Worker instead of Cloudflare's error 1033
while your computer is off. [Offline page](/docs/guides/offline-page)

**Ownership.** Teitunnel's record of what it created on Cloudflare (DNS records, logins,
rules, Workers). Only what it owns is ever changed or deleted without asking.
[DNS ownership](/docs/concepts/dns-ownership)

**Pause.** Taking a share or route on your domain offline while keeping its address and DNS
record; visitors see a paused page until you resume it.
[Pause and resume](/docs/guides/sharing#pause-and-resume)

**Plan.** The list of steps a change will make on Cloudflare, shown before anything
happens. Every change from the app, the command line, a project file or an agent is a
plan first. [How changes are made](/docs/concepts/changes)

**Private network.** A range of addresses on this computer's network that people signed in
to your Zero Trust organization with WARP can reach through your tunnel.
[Private networks](/docs/guides/private-networks)

**Project file.** A `teitunnel.yml` in a repository that describes routes, shares,
Snapshots and local domains, applied with `teitunnel up` or from the app.
[Project files](/docs/guides/project-file)

## Q–S

**Quick Share.** A share of a local service or folder. By default at a random
`trycloudflare.com` address through Cloudflare's Quick Tunnels, with no account; it can also
go on your own domain. [Share a local service](/docs/getting-started/quick-share)

**Reservation.** A hostname held for a teammate on a shared account: a placeholder DNS record
with the holder's name and an end date. [Previews in CI and teams](/docs/guides/ci-previews#teams-sharing-one-account)

**Route.** A hostname on your domain (optionally with a path) that sends visitors to a local
service through a tunnel. It stays until you remove it.
[Routes and tunnels](/docs/concepts/routes-and-tunnels)

**Run token.** The secret a connector uses to run a tunnel. Kept in your keychain and never
put on a command line. [Security](/docs/reference/security)

**Schedule.** Days and hours when a share or route on your domain is online; outside them
it's paused. [On a schedule](/docs/guides/sharing#on-a-schedule)

**Service token.** A client id and secret that let a machine (CI, a script, a server)
through a hostname's login. The secret is shown once and never stored.
[Service tokens](/docs/guides/protection#service-tokens-for-machines)

**Share on your domain.** A Quick Share at a hostname on your own domain instead of a
random address: same URL every time, removed when the share stops.
[On your own domain](/docs/getting-started/quick-share#on-your-own-domain)

**Sign in with Cloudflare.** Connecting an account through Cloudflare's own sign-in and
consent screen, with nothing to paste. [Accounts and permissions](/docs/guides/accounts#the-consent-screen)

**Snapshot.** A static copy of a folder, build or running site, published to your
Cloudflare account as a Worker, online while your computer sleeps.
[Snapshots](/docs/guides/snapshots)

## T–Z

**Tap.** The inspector's local listener between cloudflared and your service. A share or
route whose traffic goes through a tap is inspected, and can be paused or protected on this
computer. [Inspector](/docs/guides/inspector)

**`teitunnel`.** The command line tool that comes with the app, and runs on its own on
servers and in Docker. Called `teitunnel-cli` before 0.2.0.
[Command line](/docs/reference/cli)

**Tunnel.** A named, long-lived connection point on your Cloudflare account that routes
point at. Teitunnel creates one per computer and account, and more if you ask.
[Routes and tunnels](/docs/concepts/routes-and-tunnels) · [Several tunnels](/docs/guides/several-tunnels)

**Undo.** The opposite of a change that just applied, offered for a few seconds afterwards
and carried out through the same plan and apply. A step that fails halfway is undone by
itself. [How changes are made](/docs/concepts/changes#undo-after-it-worked)

**WARP.** Cloudflare's device client. People signed in to your Zero Trust organization with
WARP can reach private networks you share. [Private networks](/docs/guides/private-networks)

**Zero Trust.** Cloudflare's product family for logins (Access), devices (WARP) and
Gateway. Free for up to 50 users; an account sets it up once in the dashboard.
[Set up Cloudflare Zero Trust](/docs/guides/accounts#set-up-cloudflare-zero-trust)
