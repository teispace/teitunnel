Teitunnel is free and open source, with no paid tier and no limits of its own on what
you share. What it builds lives on **your Cloudflare account**, so Cloudflare's plans and
limits apply. Most people never leave Cloudflare's free plans. This page lists, feature by
feature, what it uses on Cloudflare, what's free, and the limits that matter.

<Callout title="Cloudflare changes its plans">
  The numbers below were checked against Cloudflare's documentation on the dates shown.
  Cloudflare's own pages are the final word; links are in each section.
</Callout>

| Feature | Cloudflare product | Free plan enough? |

## Quick Shares

A Quick Share with a random `trycloudflare.com` address uses Cloudflare's Quick Tunnels:
free, with no account. They're meant for testing:

- At most **200 requests in flight** at once; more get **429**.
- **No Server-Sent Events**, so event streams, MCP servers over SSE and some live-reload
  setups don't work. `teitunnel share --mcp` needs `--on` for this reason.
- A **new random address** every time, and no uptime guarantee.
- A brand-new address looked up before it exists can be cached as missing by a resolver
  for up to 30 minutes.

For any of these, share on your own domain instead (**Address ▸ On** *your domain*, or
`teitunnel share 3000 --on demo.teispace.com`): same free plan, same URL each time.

Checked 2026-09-24. Source:
[Quick Tunnels](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/connections/connect-networks/do-more-with-tunnels/trycloudflare/).

## Cloudflare Tunnel and routes

Cloudflare Tunnel is free on every plan, with no charge for traffic through it. A route
needs a domain whose nameservers point to Cloudflare; the free plan is enough. Limits that
apply to every request through a tunnel:

| Limit | Free and Pro | What you see |
|---|---|---|
| Time to answer | 125 seconds (only Enterprise zones can raise it) | error 524 |
| Request body size | 100 MB | error 413 |
| Free certificate | one level of subdomain (`app.teispace.com`, not `a.b.teispace.com`) | SSL handshake error |

Checked 2026-09-26 (the timeout was 100 seconds before). Source:
[connection limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/fundamentals/reference/connection-limits/),
[error 524](https://developers.cloudflare.com/support/troubleshooting/http-status-codes/cloudflare-5xx-errors/error-524/).

See [What is Cloudflare Tunnel?](/docs/concepts/cloudflare-tunnel#limits-worth-knowing) and
[Troubleshooting](/docs/reference/troubleshooting).

## Logins, service tokens and private networks

Logins, service tokens and private networks use Cloudflare Zero Trust, which is **free for
up to 50 users**. An account has to set it up once in the dashboard (a team name and the
Free plan); Teitunnel shows **Set up Cloudflare Zero Trust** when it's needed.

| Limit | Per account |
|---|---|
| Service tokens | 50 |
| Access applications | 500 |
| Reusable Access policies | 500 |

Checked 2026-09-24. Source:
[Cloudflare One account limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/account-limits/),
[service tokens](https://developers.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-one/access-controls/service-credentials/service-tokens/).

## Snapshots

A Snapshot is a Worker with static assets on your account. Requests for its files are
**free and unlimited**; the Worker's script only runs when the Snapshot has a password
or comments, and then each request counts against Workers' daily requests.

| Limit | Free | Workers Paid |
|---|---|---|
| Files per version | 20,000 | 100,000 |
| Size of one file | 25 MiB | 25 MiB |
| Workers per account | 100 | 500 |
| Requests that run a Worker | 100,000 a day | billed |
| Custom domains per zone | 100 | 100 |

Teitunnel checks the file count and sizes against the free limits before uploading.
Checked 2026-09-24. Source:
[Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/).

## Offline page and webhook inbox

Both are Workers in front of a route's hostname, so **every request to that hostname runs
the Worker**, even while your computer is on.

- Workers on the free plan run for **100,000 requests a day per account**, reset at 00:00
  UTC.
- Teitunnel creates their routes to **fail open**: past the limit, requests go straight to
  your tunnel as if no Worker were there. Your site keeps working; only the offline page, or
  keeping webhooks while you're offline, pauses until the next day.
- The plan shows this cost before you turn either on, and both are off unless you choose
  them for a route.

The inbox itself keeps at most **1,000 webhooks** of up to **512 KB** each for 1 to 30 days
(you choose), and answers 503 with `Retry-After` when full.

Checked 2026-09-25. Sources:
[Workers limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/),
[Workers routes](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/configuration/routing/routes/).

## Comments and inbox storage (D1)

Snapshot comments and webhook inboxes share one D1 database on your account,
`teitunnel-data`.

| Limit | Free |
|---|---|
| Databases per account | 10 |
| Size of one database | 500 MB |
| Storage per account | 5 GB |
| Rows read | 5 million a day |
| Rows written | 100,000 a day |

Past a daily limit, Cloudflare refuses queries until it resets. Teitunnel adds its own
limits to comments: 2,000 per share or Snapshot, 200 per thread, and 10 a minute (60 an
hour) from one visitor. Comments on live shares are kept on your computer and use no D1.

Checked 2026-09-25. Source: [D1 limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/d1/platform/limits/).

## Edge protection

Rules are always scoped to one hostname and count against your domain's plan:

| Limit per domain | Free | Pro | Business |
|---|---|---|---|
| Custom rules (bots, AI crawlers) | 5 | 20 | 100 |
| Rate limiting rules | 1 | 2 | 5 |
| Rate limiting can match a hostname | **No** | Yes | Yes |
| Rate limiting period | 10 s | up to 1 min | up to 10 min |
| Transform Rules (headers) | 10 | 25 | 50 |

On Free, a rate limiting rule can only match paths, so it would apply to the whole domain.
Teitunnel never changes a whole domain, so it refuses rate limiting on Free and explains
why. Bot and header rules work on Free. The review shows how much of your plan's quota a
change uses.

Checked 2026-09-24. Sources:
[custom rules](https://developers.cloudflare.com/waf/custom-rules/),
[rate limiting rules](https://developers.cloudflare.com/waf/rate-limiting-rules/),
[Transform Rules](https://developers.cloudflare.com/rules/transform/).

## Analytics and uptime

Traffic per hostname comes from Cloudflare's GraphQL Analytics, which is included in every
plan:

- How far back you can look depends on your domain's plan. Teitunnel reads the limit from
  Cloudflare and says from when data is available.
- Numbers come from sampled data, so at high volume they're estimates.
- Cloudflare allows 300 analytics queries per 5 minutes per user. Teitunnel caches answers
  and stays under 200 per account.

Uptime checks run on your computer, every minute, while the app, `teitunnel up` or
`teitunnel serve` runs. Each check is one request to your route through Cloudflare. Raw
checks are kept for 2 days and hourly totals for 30 days, on your computer.

Checked 2026-09-24. Source:
[GraphQL Analytics limits](https://developers.cloudflare.com/analytics/graphql-api/limits/).

## Load balancing

Load balancing across machines uses Cloudflare Load Balancing, a **paid add-on**. Turn it
on in the Cloudflare dashboard first; Teitunnel then sets up the monitor, pool and load
balancer. See [Load balancing](/docs/guides/load-balancing).

## On your computer

The inspector, local HTTPS domains, the exposure check, backups and the Doctor use nothing
on Cloudflare. The inspector keeps up to 5,000 requests per share or route (256 MB in all)
for a day by default, and up to 1 MiB of each body.
