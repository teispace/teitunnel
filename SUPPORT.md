# Getting help

## Documentation

Most answers are in the [documentation](https://teitunnel.teispace.com/docs/): the getting
started guides, a guide for each feature, the CLI reference, and
[troubleshooting](https://teitunnel.teispace.com/docs/reference/troubleshooting/) for common
errors such as 502, 530 or 1033. In the app, the **Doctor** finds most problems and offers a
fix.

## Questions

Ask in [GitHub Discussions ▸ Q&A](https://github.com/teispace/teitunnel/discussions/categories/q-a).
Search first: someone may have asked already. Say which version and operating system you
use, what you tried and what happened.

## Bugs and feature requests

- A bug: open a [bug report](https://github.com/teispace/teitunnel/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml).
- An idea: start a discussion in [Ideas](https://github.com/teispace/teitunnel/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Security issues

Never in public. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Cloudflare accounts and billing

Teitunnel is an independent project. For your Cloudflare account, plan, billing or
Cloudflare's services themselves, contact [Cloudflare support](https://support.cloudflare.com).
