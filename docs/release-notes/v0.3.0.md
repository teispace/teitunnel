# Teitunnel 0.3.0

Tunnels now look after themselves after sleep and network changes, the inspector gains
breakpoints and a live Overview, every share and route gets its own traffic numbers, and
AI tools such as claude.ai and ChatGPT can sign in to MCP servers you share.

## Highlights

- **Recovers by itself.** After your computer wakes, changes Wi-Fi or comes back online,
  tunnels reconnect within seconds instead of waiting out a retry. A tunnel that kept
  failing while you were offline is tried again automatically, and "down" notifications
  wait a moment after a wake so they don't cry wolf.
- **A live Overview.** Traffic, errors and uptime at a glance, with the latest requests as
  they arrive.
- **Breakpoints in the inspector.** Hold a request or its answer, change it, answer it
  yourself or drop it, then let it go on.
- **WebSocket messages, readable.** Filter by direction, search, and open a message to read
  it whole, with JSON laid out and a copy button. The request list stays smooth with
  10,000 requests.
- **Numbers for every share and route.** Requests per second, status codes, countries,
  browsers and bots, including Quick Shares, which Cloudflare's analytics never see.
- **Pages that load but are broken.** After you share, Teitunnel reads the page and names
  links visitors can't follow (to `localhost`, or over plain `http://`) with the setting to
  change for Laravel, Rails, Django, Flask, FastAPI or Node.
- **Share MCP servers with claude.ai and ChatGPT.** They sign in, and you approve each
  connection in Teitunnel after comparing a short code. **Settings ▸ AI Tools** lists them
  and disconnects them.
- **Pause, schedule and protect from where you are.** Pause or resume a Quick Share from
  its card, pause and schedule routes from the Routes view, and add a password or secret
  link to a Quick Share with one click.
- **Webhooks skip a route's login.** Keep a login on your site while `/webhooks` stays
  reachable for Stripe or GitHub.
- **Browser extension.** Share the local page you're on from Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc,
  Vivaldi or Firefox. The app side is in this release (**Settings ▸ Integrations ▸
  Browser Extension**); the store listings follow.
- **More for AI agents.** Test how an app copes with slow networks and failures, read a
  route's health, give a dev server a local HTTPS address, and manage offline pages and
  webhook inboxes.

What's new, with a link to each guide: https://teitunnel.teispace.com/docs/getting-started/whats-new

## Fixes

- Settings opens on Windows.
- On macOS, the app, the `teitunnel` command and AI agents share keychain items, so there
  are no repeated password prompts; credentials are read once, not on every call.
- Removing a hostname's last route, or deleting a tunnel, also removes what was attached to
  it (offline page, inbox, edge rules, service tokens, pauses, schedules), and the Doctor
  finds anything earlier versions left behind.
- Undoing a change restores a signature-checking webhook inbox with its secret.
- Folder shares and brand-new addresses work on the first try; servers running without the
  app clean up shares left behind.
- The app does its disk and network work off the main thread, and polls Cloudflare less.

## Install

Download Teitunnel for your system from **https://teitunnel.teispace.com/download**, or
update from the app (**Restart to Update**). Also:

- macOS: `brew install --cask teispace/tap/teitunnel`
- Servers: the `teitunnel` archives below, the apt/dnf repository, or the Docker image
  `teispace/teitunnel` (also `ghcr.io/teispace/teitunnel`).

Every download is listed in `SHA256SUMS.txt` and carries GitHub build provenance
(`gh attestation verify <file> --repo teispace/teitunnel`).

## Known limitations

- Windows builds are not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC":
  choose **More info ▸ Run anyway**.
- The browser extension isn't in the Chrome Web Store or Firefox Add-ons yet.
